# Code of Conduct

Comments on diogocouto.dev are hosted in this repository's Discussions. To keep them useful
and welcoming:

- Be respectful. Disagree with ideas, not people.
- Stay on topic and relevant to the post.
- No spam, advertising, or self-promotion unrelated to the post.
- No harassment, discrimination, hate speech, or personal attacks.
- Do not post private information about others or secrets such as tokens and passwords.

## Enforcement

The repository owner ([@diogocouto18](https://github.com/diogocouto18)) may edit, hide or
delete any comment and block accounts that break these rules, without prior notice. To report
a problem, use "Report content" on the comment or contact the owner.
